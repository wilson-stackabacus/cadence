Everything in one place

A planner that keeps you honest

Calendars to see the shape of your week, a checklist to focus today, and gentle nudges until it's done.

Week & month calendars

See everything at a glance, stack overlapping tasks, and double-click any slot to add one.

Recurring tasks

Daily, weekdays, every few weeks, monthly or yearly — with end dates, skips, and reminders mid-task.

Reminders that find you

Notifications, on-screen banners or sounds, a nudge every 30 minutes, and a check-in whenever you open your computer.

Reflect as you go

Checking something off asks for a short reflection. Over time they add up to a record of how you work best.

Google Calendar & Calendly

Your events and booked meetings land on your checklist automatically, and quietly, with no extra pings.

Mac and web, in sync

Use Cadence in any browser or as a Mac app. Changes sync in seconds, and it works offline too.

How it works

Three steps, every day

Small, consistent habits beat perfect plans.

Plan

Add today's tasks and set the ones that repeat. Connect your calendars to pull in meetings.

Do

Cadence reminds you at the right moments and checks in when you sit back down at your computer.

Reflect

Write a few words when you finish. See your streak grow and learn what actually works for you.

Download

Cadence on your computer

Desktop apps keep reminders running in the background and check in whenever you open your computer. Sign in with the same account and everything stays in sync.

Mac

Native app for macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon and Intel.

Download for Mac First launch: if macOS can't verify it, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.

Windows

Windows 10 and 11. Lives in the system tray and starts with Windows.

Download for Windows If SmartScreen appears, click More info › Run anyway.

Linux

AppImage for any distro, or a .deb for Debian and Ubuntu.

AppImage .deb
AppImage: run chmod +x Cadence.AppImage, then open it.

Or just use it in your browser: open the web app. All releases are on GitHub.
