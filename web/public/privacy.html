Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

The short version

  • We store what you put into Cadence (tasks, reflections, settings) so it can sync between your devices. That's it.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.
  • Google Calendar and Calendly are optional. If you connect Google Calendar, Cadence reads your events to show them, and creates, edits or deletes events only when you ask it to. Section 3 explains exactly what we access and why. You can disconnect anytime.
  • You can download all your data or delete your account in Settings › Account & sync.

This policy explains how Cadence (“Cadence”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Cadence website at cadenceplanner.vercel.app and the Cadence app for Mac (together, the “Service”). Questions? Email cadencemain26@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your username and password. We never store your password itself, only a one-way scrypt hash of it, which can't be turned back into the password.
  • Your content: tasks and to-dos (titles, notes, dates, times, repeat schedules, reminder choices, colors, and which ones you checked off), the reflections you write, and your Cadence settings (reminder preferences, open hours, and whether each item is “open” or “closed” time).
  • Things you type into booking forms: if you book a time and add an invitee, their name and email address are sent to Google Calendar so Google can send them the invitation. Cadence doesn't keep the email address; the name stays only if you put it in the event's title.

Information created when you use the Service

  • Sign-in sessions: when you sign in we create a random session token. We store only a hash of it, with the time it was created and last used and whether it came from the web or the Mac app.
  • Security records: to stop password guessing, we record failed sign-in attempts by username and IP address. These records are cleared after 15 minutes.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider, Vercel, keeps short-lived technical request logs (such as IP address, time, and page requested) to operate and secure the Service.

Information from services you connect (optional)

  • Google Calendar: if you connect it, we receive data from your Google Account as described in detail in section 3.
  • Calendly: if you connect it, we store the Personal Access Token you paste in, plus your Calendly name, profile link, and booking links. With it we read your scheduled meetings, including invitee names, and your event types.

Events and meetings imported from these services become tasks in your Cadence account, so they're stored like your other content.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: save your data, sync it between your devices, show your calendars, send the reminders you set up, and import your events.
  • To keep the Service secure: authenticate you, prevent abuse, and investigate problems.
  • To respond when you contact us.

We don't use your information for advertising, profiling, or selling, and we don't use it to train AI models.

3. Google user data

This section applies when you choose to connect Google Calendar in Cadence (on the website, or in the Mac app). Connecting is optional; everything else in Cadence works without it.

3.1 What we access

When you connect, Google asks you to approve these permissions (“scopes”):

PermissionWhat Cadence accesses with it
calendar.readonly (“See and download any calendar you can access”)The list of your calendars (name, color, and whether it's your primary calendar); events on the calendars you choose: title, start and end time, all-day flag, description, location, link to the event, whether it repeats, whether it's marked “busy” or “free”, and whether it was cancelled; and free/busy times for those calendars.
calendar.events (“View and edit events on all your calendars”)Creating an event when you ask Cadence to (a new event set to sync with Google, “Also add to Google Calendar” on a task, or a booking), editing an event you edit in Cadence (title, notes, date, time, busy/free), deleting an event when you choose “Delete from Google too”, and subscribing to change notifications so edits you make in Google appear in Cadence quickly.
email (website only)Your Google Account email address, shown in Settings so you can see which Google Account is connected.

We don't access your Gmail, contacts, Drive, or any other Google data. Cadence never creates, changes, or deletes anything in your Google Calendar unless you take an action in Cadence that asks it to.

3.2 How we use it

We use Google user data only to provide and improve user-facing features you can see in Cadence:

  • Showing your Google events on your Cadence calendars, and reminding you about them if you turn that on.
  • Importing events into your Cadence account as calendar items, so they show on every device you use Cadence on and stay up to date when they change in Google.
  • Working out your open time on the Booking page from your busy times.
  • Creating, updating, and deleting the specific events you ask Cadence to.

We don't use Google user data for advertising, retargeting, or interest-based ads; we don't sell it; we don't use it to build user profiles or for credit or lending decisions; and we don't use it to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

3.3 How we store and protect it

  • Connection tokens: Google's access and refresh tokens, your Google email, the calendars you selected, and your time zone are stored in our database (Turso), on our servers only. The website never sends your refresh token to your browser. If you connect Google directly in the Mac app instead, its tokens are stored on your Mac in a private file only your macOS user account can read.
  • Imported events: the title, notes (taken from the event description, or its location), date, time, length, busy/free setting, event link, and Google event and calendar IDs are stored with your other Cadence content, in our database and on the devices you use Cadence on.
  • Not stored: free/busy times and the events Cadence only displays (without importing) are fetched when needed and not saved in our database.
  • Change notifications: to receive Google's “something changed” notifications, we store a random channel ID and token, the calendar ID, and when the subscription expires. The notifications themselves contain no event details.
  • Protection: all data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. Access to the database is restricted to the Cadence server with secret credentials, and all requests require your signed-in session. See also section 9.

3.4 Sharing

We don't share, transfer, or disclose Google user data to third parties, except: to the service providers that run Cadence for us and only to provide the features above (Vercel for hosting and Turso for the database, see section 4); when required by law; or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you. When you book a time with an invitee, the invitee's details are sent to Google, at your request, so Google can invite them.

3.5 Human access

No person reads your Google user data unless you give us permission for a specific item (for example, when asking for support), it's necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, it's required by law, or it has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

3.6 Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting (Settings › Google Calendar › Disconnect): we stop the change-notification subscriptions, ask Google to revoke Cadence's access, and immediately delete your Google tokens, email, and calendar choices from our database. Events already imported stay in your Cadence account as ordinary items until you delete them; you can delete any of them yourself.
  • Revoking from Google: you can also remove Cadence at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Cadence then can no longer access your calendar, and you can disconnect in Settings to delete the stored tokens.
  • Deleting your account (Settings › Account & sync › Delete account) permanently deletes all of it, including imported events and Google tokens, from our database right away. Residual copies may remain in our database provider's backups for a limited period (typically no more than 30 days) until they're overwritten.
  • You can ask us to delete your Google user data at any time by emailing cadencemain26@gmail.com; we'll do so within 30 days.

3.7 Limited Use

Cadence's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. On your devices

  • Cookies: the website uses one essential cookie, cadence_session, to keep you signed in. If you choose “Keep me signed in”, it lasts up to a year, renewing while you use Cadence. Otherwise it ends when you close your browser. We don't use any other cookies.
  • Browser storage: the website keeps a copy of your data and a few preferences for this browser (like whether to show reminders here) in your browser's local storage, so it loads fast and works offline. Signing out or deleting your account clears it.
  • Notifications: reminders are created on your device. If you allow browser or macOS notifications, the operating system displays them; we don't use a push-notification service to send them.
  • Mac app: your data is saved on your Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/Cadence, and sign-in tokens are kept in a private file there that only your macOS user account can read. The Mac app talks to Google and Calendly directly if you connect them there. It only sends your data to our servers if you sign in to sync.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data and content: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Items you delete are removed from the Service. A small record that the item was deleted (its ID and time, without its content) may be kept so the deletion syncs to your other devices. Imported calendar items you delete are hidden rather than removed, so they don't come back on the next import. Deleting your account removes them.
  • Sessions: until they expire (up to one year of inactivity) or you sign out.
  • Failed sign-in records: 15 minutes.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and portability: download all your data as a file in Settings › Account & sync › Download my data.
  • Correction: edit any task, reflection, or setting directly in the app.
  • Deletion: delete your account in Settings › Account & sync › Delete account. This permanently erases your account and content from our database and disconnects Google and Calendly. Data on your own devices, such as the Mac app's local file, stays there until you delete it.
  • Disconnecting services: disconnect Google Calendar or Calendly at any time in Settings.

Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Economic Area), you may have additional rights, such as to know what we hold about you, object to certain uses, or complain to a data-protection authority. Email cadencemain26@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. We don't sell or “share” personal information as those terms are defined under California law.
